# Lake Julian Park

300-acre Buncombe County park on a "thermal" lake (used as a power-plant cooling reservoir) with excellent bass/catfish/crappie/tilapia fishing (best Oct-March), paddle boat/canoe rentals, six picnic shelters, a playground, and the annual Festival of Lights (December).
