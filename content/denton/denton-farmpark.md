# Denton FarmPark

Family-owned heritage park in Denton with restored historic buildings, antique equipment, train rides, camping, and multiple annual festivals.

## Logistics

- **Nearest town folder:** denton
- **County:** Davidson
- **Location:** 1072 Cranford Rd, Denton, NC 27239 (main entrance); back entrance at 4259 Handy Rd
- **Seasonality:** Year-round venue with recurring seasonal events.

## Notes

The park includes a cluster of restored and relocated historic structures (general store, church, train station, plantation house, and more), working demonstrations, and event infrastructure that supports camping, vendors, and large public gatherings.
