# Southeast Old Threshers Reunion

Flagship annual Denton FarmPark event with antique machinery, train rides, live demonstrations, vendors, and family activities.

## Logistics

- **Nearest town folder:** denton
- **County:** Davidson
- **Venue/location:** Denton FarmPark, 1072 Cranford Rd, Denton, NC 27239
- **Timing:** Annual, held during the week of the 4th of July (exact dates vary by year)
- **Cost:** Ticketed event; check current-year admission and camping details.

## Notes

Official event copy describes this as the park's founding tradition (started in 1970), featuring restored historical buildings, an hourly cowboy-and-bandit train show, a daily "Parade of Power" at 4:00 PM, and hands-on demonstrations across the grounds.
