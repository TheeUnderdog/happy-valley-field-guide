# WNC Agricultural Center

87-acre state-owned facility near Asheville Regional Airport; home of the NC Mountain State Fair (see separate event entry) and year-round horse/livestock shows, car shows, rodeos, equestrian competitions, and motocross. Includes the Morris L. "Mac" McGough Arena (year-round indoor venue, ~3,000-seat capacity, 28,800 sq ft dirt floor) and a LEED-certified Event/Expo Center (27,000+ sq ft).

## Logistics

- **Nearest town folder:** fletcher
- **County:** Henderson
- **Venue/location:** 1301 Fanning Bridge Rd, Fletcher, NC 28732 (Davis Event Center offices at 765 Boylston Hwy)
- **Direction:** ~12 miles south of downtown Asheville, adjacent to Asheville Regional Airport

## Notes

The McGough Arena hosts rodeos, horse shows, and livestock competitions outside of fair season, but a specific recurring named show/date could not be confirmed this pass — check the facility-rental page or call ahead for the current calendar.

Domain notes: `mountainstatefair.com` redirects to a carnival-ride vendor's site (not the fair) — a hijacked/repurposed domain. Use `wncagcenter.org` as the correct official site instead; it's a heavy JavaScript ticketing platform, so plan to browse it directly rather than relying on a plain page fetch.
