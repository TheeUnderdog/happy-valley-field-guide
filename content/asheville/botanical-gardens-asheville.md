# Botanical Gardens at Asheville

10-acre native-plant garden adjacent to UNC Asheville with a half-mile loop trail, the historic "dog trot" Hayes Cabin, and 50+ rare/endangered native species.

## Logistics

- **Nearest town folder:** asheville
- **County:** Buncombe
- **Venue/location:** 151 W.T. Weaver Blvd, Asheville, NC 28804
- **Phone:** (828) 252-5190
- **Cost:** Free
- **Dog-friendly note:** no — the gardens explicitly ask visitors not to bring pets, radios, or sports equipment

## Notes

Despite being free and scenic, this is NOT a dog-friendly stop — plan accordingly if bringing a dog on the trip.
