# Biltmore Estate

George Vanderbilt's 1895 250-room Gilded Age chateau, America's largest privately owned home, with Frederick Law Olmsted-designed gardens, a conservatory, winery, and miles of estate trails/equestrian and biking roads.

## Logistics

- **Nearest town folder:** asheville
- **County:** Buncombe
- **Venue/location:** 1 Lodge St, Asheville, NC 28803 (Antler Hill Village entrance area; estate spans ~8,000 acres)
- **Dog-friendly note:** leashed dogs allowed in many outdoor garden/grounds areas; not inside the house — verify current policy before visiting

## Notes

Paid admission attraction (not free). Includes house tour, gardens, winery, Antler Hill Village, and estate trails for hiking/biking/horseback riding.
