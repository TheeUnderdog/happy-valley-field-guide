# Mount Pisgah Trail and Pisgah Inn (Blue Ridge Parkway)

Moderately strenuous 1.5-mile one-way hike to the 5,721-ft summit of Mount Pisgah with panoramic views of Cold Mountain; Pisgah Inn (open April-October) is the only full-service lodge/restaurant directly on the North Carolina Parkway.

## Logistics

- **Nearest town folder:** asheville
- **County:** Buncombe (trailhead near the Haywood/Buncombe line, ~26 miles southwest of downtown Asheville)
- **Venue/location:** Blue Ridge Parkway Milepost 407.6 (trailhead); Pisgah Inn, Milepost 408.6
- **Dog-friendly note:** leashed dogs allowed on Parkway trails
- **Seasonality:** Pisgah Inn open April-October

## Notes

A farther drive down the Parkway from Asheville proper, but a strong scenic/hiking anchor for a longer day trip.
