# Asheville City Market

ASAP (Appalachian Sustainable Agriculture Project)-run producer-only farmers market in downtown Asheville; accepts cash, card, and SNAP/EBT via market tokens.

## Logistics

- **Nearest town folder:** asheville
- **County:** Buncombe
- **Venue/location:** N. Market Street (near the Thomas Wolfe Center), downtown Asheville, NC
- **Timing:** Every Saturday, year-round

## Notes

Part of the WNC Double SNAP Network (ASAP + Blue Ridge Women in Agriculture + MountainWise), which provides Farm Fresh Bucks matching at participating farmers markets.
