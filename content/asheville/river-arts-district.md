# River Arts District (RAD)

Former industrial riverfront (Cotton Mill, Nabisco warehouse) along the French Broad River, now home to 700+ working artist studios/galleries, breweries (Wedge Brewing at 37 Paynes Way & 5 Foundy St), and the Saturday Art Market at Pinegate Renewables.

Rebuilding post-Hurricane Helene (Sept 2024); largely reopened as of 2026 with some studios relocated. The domain riverartsdistrict.com hit a redirect loop during research — verify manually before relying on it as a source; the studio directory itself is a legitimate ongoing organization.
