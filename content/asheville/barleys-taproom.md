# Barley's Taproom (in place of Salvage Station)

Downtown Asheville beer bar/taproom; currently hosting some of the Salvage Station team's programming while that venue is closed.

## Logistics

- **Nearest town folder:** asheville
- **County:** Buncombe
- **Venue/location:** 42 Biltmore Avenue, Asheville, NC 28801

## Notes

Important status flag: **Salvage Station** (the well-known River Arts District-area live music venue) is currently **closed** — its own site states it suffered extensive Hurricane Helene damage and lost its 20-year lease to the I-26 connector highway project, with no announced reopening. Its team pointed fans to Barley's Taproom "in the meantime." Do not plan a visit to Salvage Station itself; verify current live-music programming at Barley's before relying on it as a direct substitute.
