# Craggy Gardens (Blue Ridge Parkway)

High-elevation (5,497-5,892 ft) grassy balds famous for Catawba rhododendron bloom in early-to-mid June, with the Craggy Pinnacle Trail (1.5 mi round trip, 360-degree views) and Craggy Gardens Trail.

## Logistics

- **Nearest town folder:** asheville
- **County:** Buncombe (~20-24 miles north of downtown)
- **Venue/location:** Blue Ridge Parkway Milepost 364.1 (Craggy Pinnacle), 364.4 (Visitor Center), 367.6 (Picnic Area)
- **Dog-friendly note:** leashed dogs allowed
- **Seasonality:** Visitor Center open late May-October only; Parkway sometimes closed in winter

## Notes

Best late spring/early summer for rhododendron bloom; also a strong fall-color overlook. Along the Parkway between Asheville and Weaverville/Barnardsville.
