# Shindig on the Green 2026 (60th Season)

Free weekly Saturday-evening tradition since 1967 with house band The Stoney Creek Boys, bluegrass/old-time string bands, big-circle and clog dancers, ballad singers, and storytellers around Pack Square Park. Draws 2,000-3,000 attendees per evening, with concessions from Bear's Smokehouse and Hop Ice Cream Cafe.
