# Grovewood Village and Estes-Winn Antique Car Museum

Historic Biltmore Industries campus adjacent to the Omni Grove Park Inn, with the Grovewood Gallery, an outdoor sculpture garden, and the Estes-Winn Antique Car Museum.

## Logistics

- **Nearest town folder:** asheville
- **County:** Buncombe
- **Venue/location:** 111 Grovewood Rd, Asheville, NC 28804
- **Phone:** (828) 253-2698 (Estes-Winn museum)
- **Cost:** Free (suggested $5 donation at the car museum)
- **Seasonality:** Car museum open April-December

## Notes

Founded in 1966 by Harry Blomberg (who also saved Thomas Wolfe's Old Kentucky Home); collection includes a 1957 Cadillac Eldorado Brougham and a 1922 American LaFrance fire truck. The museum celebrated its 60th anniversary June 13, 2026 (see the related past event entry).
