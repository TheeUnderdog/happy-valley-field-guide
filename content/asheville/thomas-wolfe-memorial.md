# Thomas Wolfe Memorial (Old Kentucky Home)

1883 Queen Anne boarding house, childhood home of novelist Thomas Wolfe and the setting for "Look Homeward, Angel"; a National Historic Landmark rebuilt after a 1998 arson fire, with guided house tours daily.
