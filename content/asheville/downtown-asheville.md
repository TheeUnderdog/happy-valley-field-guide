# Downtown Asheville (Grove Arcade, Pack Square, Lexington Ave, Wall St)

Historic downtown core: the 1929 Grove Arcade, Pack Square Park (splash fountain, Urban Trail sculptures), and the Haywood St / Wall St / Lexington Ave shops, galleries, bookstores, and taprooms.

## Logistics

- **Nearest town folder:** asheville
- **County:** Buncombe
- **Venue/location:** Centered on Pack Square, 2 South Pack Sq area, Asheville, NC 28801
- **Cost:** Free to walk/browse
- **Dog-friendly note:** outdoor plazas generally dog-friendly; Grove Arcade interior is not pet-friendly

## Notes

Good baseline first stop for downtown orientation, shopping, food, art walks, and people-watching. Home base for several recurring free events (Downtown After 5, Shindig on the Green, Mountain Dance and Folk Festival — see separate event entries).
