# Mountain Dance and Folk Festival 2026 (99th Annual)

The oldest continuously running folk festival in the US, founded in 1928 by Bascom Lamar Lunsford and run since 1967 by the Folk Heritage Committee. Features old-time/bluegrass string bands, clogging, big-circle mountain dance, ballad singers, and storytellers.
