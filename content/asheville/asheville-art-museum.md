# Asheville Art Museum

AAM-accredited museum in a renovated 1925 Italian Renaissance-style former library building (54,000 sq ft since a 2019 expansion), focused on 20th/21st-century American art, Studio Craft, Black Mountain College, and Cherokee artists.
