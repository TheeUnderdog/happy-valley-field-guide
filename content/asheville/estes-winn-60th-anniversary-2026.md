# Estes-Winn Antique Car Museum 60th Anniversary Celebration

Free community celebration of the Estes-Winn Antique Car Museum's 60th anniversary, honoring founder Harry D. Blomberg (who also saved Thomas Wolfe's Old Kentucky Home). Included a guided Biltmore Industries history tour, a historian talk, and viewing of the permanent collection.
