# Folk Art Center (Blue Ridge Parkway)

Home of the Southern Highland Craft Guild (chartered 1930): three galleries of Appalachian folk art/craft, daily craft demonstrations, and the oldest continuously operating craft shop in the US (since 1897). The most-visited Blue Ridge Parkway attraction (~250,000 visitors/year).
