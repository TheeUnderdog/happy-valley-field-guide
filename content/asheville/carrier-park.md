# Carrier Park

Former racetrack/velodrome converted to a city park with volleyball, a playground, a roller-hockey rink, lawn bowling, and river overlooks; anchors the French Broad River Greenway (2.8 mi paved trail to Hominy Creek Park and French Broad River Park).
