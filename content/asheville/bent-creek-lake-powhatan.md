# Bent Creek Experimental Forest / Lake Powhatan Recreation Area

~30 miles of mountain-biking trails in Pisgah National Forest adjacent to the NC Arboretum; Lake Powhatan has a swimming beach, fishing pier, and campground (open March-November).
