# Biltmore Village

Historic cottage shopping/dining district built by Vanderbilt (architect Richard Sharp Smith) across from the Biltmore Estate entrance, with 40+ shops/galleries, restaurants, and breweries.

## Logistics

- **Nearest town folder:** asheville
- **County:** Buncombe
- **Venue/location:** Historic district across from the Biltmore Estate entrance, centered on Hendersonville Rd/All Souls Crescent, Asheville, NC 28803
- **Dog-friendly note:** outdoor sidewalks/areas generally dog-friendly; individual shop policies vary

## Notes

Free to walk/shop (Biltmore Estate itself is separate paid admission next door). Flooded in 1916 and again in September 2024 (Hurricane Helene); largely reopened as of 2026 with some displaced businesses. The domain biltmorevillage.com is not an independent visitor guide (it redirects to a shopping-center/leasing company site) — don't cite it as an authoritative source.
