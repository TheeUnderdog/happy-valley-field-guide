# ASAP Farm Tour 2026

Self-guided driving tour of diverse working farms across the region — vegetables, livestock, vineyards, orchards, flowers, fiber. One vehicle pass covers all farms both days. Strong fit for agriculture/tractors/horses/photography interests.

## Logistics

- **Nearest town folder:** asheville (ASAP is headquartered here; participating farms are regional across WNC and may include farms well outside Asheville itself)
- **County:** Buncombe (regional)
- **Venue/location:** Multiple working farms within ~1 hour of Asheville (specific farms announced closer to the date)
- **Timing:** September 19-20, 2026, noon-5pm
- **Cost:** $35 advance / $45 day-of per vehicle (covers all farms both days)

## Notes

This runs the same weekend as the Weaverville "Art in Autumn" festival (also Sept 19, 2026) — worth planning as a combined weekend if interested in both.
