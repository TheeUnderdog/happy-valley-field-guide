# North Carolina Arboretum

434-acre UNC-system arboretum within the Bent Creek Experimental Forest, near Blue Ridge Parkway Milepost 393.6, with a bonsai collection, Blue Ridge Quilt Garden, National Native Azalea Repository, and extensive hiking/biking trails.

## Logistics

- **Nearest town folder:** asheville
- **County:** Buncombe
- **Venue/location:** 100 Frederick Law Olmsted Way, Asheville, NC 28806
- **Cost:** Free admission; parking fee applies

## Notes

Hosts the annual Winter Lights holiday display (see separate event entry) and other seasonal programming.
