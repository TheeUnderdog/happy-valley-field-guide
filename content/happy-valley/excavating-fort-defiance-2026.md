# Excavating Fort Defiance

Public archaeology event at Fort Defiance where visitors can watch the stockade fort excavation and help screen dirt for artifacts.

## Logistics

- **Nearest town folder:** happy-valley
- **County:** Caldwell
- **Venue/location:** Fort Defiance, 1792 Fort Defiance Drive, Lenoir, NC 28645
- **Timing:** October 3-4, 2026, starting at 10:00am
- **Cost:** Free event; optional tour of William Lenoir's historic home uses standard admission.

## Notes

Dr. Ellen Cowan of Appalachian State University's geology department discovered the fort site in 2023. The event page says visitors may come and go, learn about the excavation process, and participate by screening dirt for artifacts that Fort Defiance will inventory and care for.
