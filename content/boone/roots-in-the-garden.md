# Roots in the Garden (Daniel Boone Native Gardens)

Acoustic folk, bluegrass, and singer-songwriter concert series on a new outdoor stage at Daniel Boone Native Gardens. Concerts pair with visual-artist showcases (artwork auctioned at the end of the evening to benefit the Gardens), food trucks, and beer/wine for purchase.

## Logistics

- **Nearest town folder:** boone
- **County:** Watauga
- **Venue/location:** Daniel Boone Native Gardens, 651 Horn in the West Drive, Boone, NC 28607
- **Timing:** Music begins 5:30pm each concert date; the series' stage held its grand opening/ribbon-cutting May 28, with the Town of Boone, Council members, and the Boone Chamber of Commerce
- **Cost:** Beer and wine available for purchase; picnics welcome
- **Dog-friendly note:** no — no dogs or glass containers permitted in the Gardens

## Notes

A newer addition to the Boone live-music scene (first season 2026). All proceeds support the Gardens' mission. Verify the current-year schedule before planning — this is the same physical place already in the guide as "Daniel Boone Native Gardens," now also hosting this concert series.
