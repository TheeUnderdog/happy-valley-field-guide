# Carolina Fly-Wheelers High Country Crank-Up 2026 (46th Annual)

46th annual antique engine/tractor show hosted by Carolina Fly-Wheelers Inc. at the Watauga County Fairgrounds, featuring antique engines, steam engines, models, tractors, tools, and other farm equipment, plus vendors and primitive camping. This is exactly the tractors/antique-power niche this guide's earlier research passes flagged as a gap for the High Country/Boone area.

## Logistics

- **Nearest town folder:** boone
- **County:** Watauga
- **Venue/location:** Watauga County Fairgrounds, 748 Roby Greene Road, Boone, NC 28607 (about 3 miles south of Boone toward North Wilkesboro)
- **Timing:** Thursday-Saturday, July 23-25, 2026; gates open 9am each day
- **Cost:** $5/day, $6 two-day pass, $8 three-day pass for the general public
- **Dog-friendly note:** unknown

## Notes

Already occurred for 2026 — this entry is kept for reference and as a strong signal the event recurs annually each July. Run by Carolina Fly-Wheelers Inc.
