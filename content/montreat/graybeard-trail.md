# Graybeard Trail (Montreat)

Strenuous ~10-mile round-trip hike (2,400 ft elevation gain) to the 5,480-ft summit of Graybeard Mountain, the highest point in Montreat, with views of the Craggies; passes the Walker's Knob overlook.
