# Montreat (village, conference-center grounds & trails)

Secluded mountain-cove village (population roughly 700) home to Montreat College and the Montreat Conference Center (PC-USA); ~30 miles of hiking trails through surrounding wilderness, plus Lake Susan for paddle boating/fishing. Billy Graham's longtime home was here.

## Logistics

- **Nearest town folder:** montreat
- **County:** Buncombe
- **Venue/location:** 401 Assembly Dr, Montreat, NC 28757 (Montreat Conference Center); village center at Lake Susan
- **Direction:** ~17 miles east of downtown Asheville, just past Black Mountain
