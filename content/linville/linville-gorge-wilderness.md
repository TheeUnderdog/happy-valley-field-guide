# Linville Gorge Wilderness

USFS wilderness known as the Grand Canyon of North Carolina, with dramatic overlooks, rugged trails, rock climbing, Table Rock, and Wisemans View.

## Logistics

- **Nearest town folder:** linville
- **County:** Burke
- **Location:** Access via NC-181, Kistler Memorial Highway, Table Rock Road, and nearby trailheads
- **Seasonality:** Year-round unless noted; verify hours before visiting.

## Notes

Some weekend use requires free permits May through October; check USFS for road/trail conditions.
