# MerleFest 2027 (39th Annual)

Doc Watson's Americana and roots music homecoming on the Wilkes Community College campus; 2027 headliners include Gillian Welch & David Rawlings, The Avett Brothers, Wynonna Judd and Toy Factory Project.

## Logistics

- **Nearest town folder:** north-wilkesboro
- **County:** Wilkes
- **Venue/location:** Wilkes Community College, 1328 S. Collegiate Drive, Wilkesboro, NC 28697
- **Timing:** April 29-May 2, 2027
- **Cost:** Ticketed; tickets on sale since Nov 10, 2026

## Notes

Other announced artists include Sam Bush, Jerry Douglas, The Earls of Leicester, Peter Rowan, Alison Brown and Donna the Buffalo.
