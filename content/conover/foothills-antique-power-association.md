# Foothills Antique Power Association of NC

Non-profit club ("Preserving the Past for the Future") dedicated to the collection, restoration, and demonstration of antique farm machinery, as well as autos, trucks, motorcycles, chainsaws, stationary engines, and other hand-powered or horse-powered equipment.

## Notes

Member-run club whose main public-facing events are held at the Hickory American Legion Fairgrounds in Newton — see the separate "Foothills Antique Power Association Fall Show & Swap Meet," "IHRA Pro Pulling League Truck & Tractor Pull," and "Foothills Antique Power Spring Show" event entries. Members also participate in area parades, festivals, and shows, including the Harvest Folk Festival at Murray's Mill (already covered in the catawba town folder). Over the past 14+ years the association has donated $150,000+ to local charities from show proceeds. No alcohol is permitted at the association's own shows.
