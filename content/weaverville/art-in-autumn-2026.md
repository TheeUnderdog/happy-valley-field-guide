# Art in Autumn (Weaverville)

19th annual juried outdoor arts and crafts festival on Main Street in downtown Weaverville, with 100+ juried artists/craftspeople and live music.

## Logistics

- **Nearest town folder:** weaverville
- **County:** Buncombe
- **Venue/location:** Main Street, Downtown Weaverville, NC 28787 (about 7 miles north of downtown Asheville)
- **Timing:** September 19, 2026, 10:00 am-6:00 pm
- **Dog-friendly note:** unknown

## Notes

Event details: over 100 juried artists and craftspeople, plus live music. The 2026 guest judge is Sarah Wells Rolland; awards include $1,000 best of show, $500 second place, $250 third place, and four $50 honorable mentions. Event t-shirts ($20) and posters ($10) are sold at the Information Booth.

2026 entertainment schedule (under the covered garage at Jonathon's on Main Street):
- Mitch Hampton — 10:30 am
- Steve Trisman and Friends — 12:00 pm
- Maddi and Clarke — 1:30 pm
- Steelin' Time — 3:00 pm

Admission cost and dog policy were not stated on the official event page; confirm before planning.
