# LEAF Festival / Lake Eden Reunion — status uncertain

**Do not plan a trip around this yet — verify current status first.**

LEAF Global Arts, the nonprofit behind the well-known twice-yearly Lake Eden Arts Festival (LEAF) held at Camp Rockmont/Lake Eden since 1995, posted an announcement (confirmed directly from theleaf.org) that it is **transitioning from an independent nonprofit into becoming part of Camp Grier**, a youth/outdoor-recreation nonprofit. The letter says LEAF Schools & Streets, LEAF International, and arts-focused summer camp programming will continue, and references a new partnership called "Lake Eden Reunion" as part of "the next chapter" — but it does **not** confirm that the prior twice-yearly music festival itself is continuing in its known form. No live Lake Eden Reunion site or 2026/2027 festival dates could be found during this research pass.
