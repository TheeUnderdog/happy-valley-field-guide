# Downtown Black Mountain (shops/galleries)

Walkable small-town downtown with 40+ shops, 30 restaurants, and 3 breweries, including Town Hardware & General Store, Tyson Furniture Company, and Seven Sisters Gallery; the Black Mountain Center for the Arts occupies the renovated Old City Hall.

- **Nearest town folder:** black-mountain

## Notes

Voted "prettiest small town" by TripAdvisor readers; ~15 miles east of downtown Asheville. Legacy of the experimental Black Mountain College (1933-1957) is marketed heavily here.
