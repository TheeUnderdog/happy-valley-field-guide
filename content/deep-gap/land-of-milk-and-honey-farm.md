# Land of Milk & Honey Farm

Christian family-owned working farm in Deep Gap offering guided farm tours, a farm-stay Airbnb, and Harvest Hosts RV stays, with beef, pork, lamb, eggs, honey, baked goods, and handmade goods for sale.

## Logistics

- **Nearest town folder:** deep-gap
- **County:** Watauga
- **Location:** Deep Gap, NC, about 1 mile off US-421, serving Boone, Deep Gap, and Blowing Rock
- **Seasonality:** Year-round; tours offered several days a week, reservation required.

## Notes

Animals on site include alpacas, Katahdin hair sheep, East Friesian milk sheep, beef cattle, Jersey milk cows, miniature donkeys, Meishan and Kunekune pasture pigs, chickens, ducks, geese, quail, and honeybees. Farm sells beef, pork, lamb, eggs, honey, baked goods/candy, beeswax candles, leather goods, and skincare. Offers a short-term farm-stay rental four houses from the farm and Harvest Hosts RV stays on-site with bathroom/shower and laundry. Verify exact street address, tour days/times, and pricing before visiting.

In addition to standard farm tours, the farm runs recurring seasonal bookable experiences (booked via Peek, not fixed calendar dates): **Lamb Cuddles**, **Goat Kid Feeding**, and **Piglet Playdates** (seasonally available, e.g. active as of Nov 2026). Check the farm's Events page and booking link for current availability before planning a visit around a specific experience.
