# Grandfather Mountain State Park

Free backcountry state park with rugged alpine-style trails, ladders, cables, ridge hiking, and dramatic views.

## Logistics

- **Nearest town folder:** grandfather-mountain
- **County:** Avery/Caldwell/Watauga
- **Location:** Profile Trail off NC-105; Boone Fork and Daniel Boone Scout Trail from BRP area
- **Seasonality:** Year-round unless noted; verify hours before visiting.

## Notes

Requires self-registration at trailheads; much harder than casual park walks.
