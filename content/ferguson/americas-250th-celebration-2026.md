# America's 250th Celebration

America's 250th Celebration at Whippoorwill Academy and Village with music, clogging, historical programs, demonstrations, vendors, food, contests, pageants, and tours of village buildings.

## Logistics

- **Nearest town folder:** ferguson
- **County:** Wilkes
- **Venue/location:** Whippoorwill Academy and Village, 11928 NC Hwy 268 W, Ferguson, NC 28624
- **Timing:** September 19, 2026, 10:00 am-5:00 pm
- **Cost:** $10 per person; children five and under free; free parking
- **Food:** Food will be available for purchase.
- **Dog-friendly note:** unknown

## Notes

The official poster lists musicians Jack Thompson and Friends, RG Absher, Michael Riggs, and jazz musicians Jesse Smith and Laura Coston; dance performances by Freda de Gruchy Foothill Cloggers; children's America 250th pageant; Daniel Boone and Martha Washington visits; films and talks; old-time toy and saw demonstrations; fiber art, spinning wheel, chair caning, blacksmith, painting, pottery, and vendor activities; a petting zoo; food vendors; and open village buildings including the bookstore, Matt's Country Store, the Whippoorwill Schoolhouse, Yadkin River Jail, Chapel of Peace, Daniel Boone Cabin, Daniel Boone Hunting Lodge, Tom Dooley Museum, and Smokehouse Gallery.

The poster notes that proceeds go to the Edith's Barn Project, a 501(c)(3).
