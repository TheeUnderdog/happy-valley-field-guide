# Music in the Valle

Long-running Friday-night summer concert series in a wide-open riverside field at Valle Crucis Community Park (a short drive from Boone, between Boone and Banner Elk), with folk, bluegrass, and family-band acts. Free and donation-based, with a very local, family-picnic feel.
