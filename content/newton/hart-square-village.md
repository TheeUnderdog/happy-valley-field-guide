# Hart Square Village

200-acre living-history village with 103 historic log structures (built 1760-1893) and 10,000+ artifacts, the nation's largest collection of preserved historic log buildings. Founded by Dr. Robert Hart III; donated in 2017 to the nonprofit Hart Square Foundation, which preserves the village and shares traditional Appalachian arts and trades.
