# Foothills Antique Power Spring Show 2027

Annual Antique Power Show hosted by the Foothills Antique Power Association, traditionally held the third weekend in May. The show includes more than tractor displays: a Friday-evening Lawn Mower Pull, a Saturday-morning Pedal Tractor Pull, and a Saturday afternoon/evening Truck & Tractor Pull, plus demonstrations of threshers, steam engines, shingle mills, grist mills, blacksmithing, and other antique equipment throughout the day. Plenty of food is available all weekend, but no alcohol is permitted; camping hookups are available.

## Notes

The association's About Us page also describes a separate annual Swap Meet "held on the third Saturday in September at the same fairgrounds" — this may be distinct from, or an older description superseded by, the October "Fall Show & Swap Meet" already in this guide (see that entry). Verify with the association which recurring show(s) are current before planning around a September date.
