# Warren Wilson College Farm & Trails

300-acre working farm and market garden plus 600 acres of managed forest with 16 miles of hiking trails, part of a work college where students run farm operations (livestock, crops), along the Swannanoa River. Also home to the Swannanoa Gathering folk/traditional-music workshop series.
